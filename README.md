# ClickSync FX 🖱️🔊
**The Ultimate Audio Engine for Your Mouse.**

**ClickSync FX** is a lightweight, high-performance application that transforms your mouse clicks into a dynamic audio experience. Unlike basic sound scripts, ClickSync FX uses a real-time audio synthesis engine (NumPy) to allow for independent pitch shifting of your Left and Right mouse buttons.

Whether you want the satisfying click of a mechanical keyboard, a custom gun sound, or a UI sound effect, ClickSync FX handles it with zero latency.

###  Key Features

*   **Real-Time Pitch FX:** A dedicated "Pitch Engine" allows you to deepen or heighten the pitch of your clicks.
    *   **LMB (Left Click):** Customize the pitch for primary actions.
    *   **RMB (Right Click):** Set a different pitch for secondary actions (e.g., ADS in games).
*   **Universal Audio Support:** Drag and drop any `.wav` or `.mp3` file.
*   **Ghost Mode:** Minimizes instantly to the System Tray (Hidden Icons) to keep your taskbar clean while it runs in the background.
*   **Master Gain Control:** Independent volume slider to mix the sound perfectly with your music or game.
*   **Zero Latency:** Built on the Pygame mixer engine for instant feedback.

---

###  How To Use

1.  **Launch:** Open `ClickSyncFX.exe`. (No installation required).
2.  **Load:** Click the **LOAD AUDIO** button and select your desired sound file (Short sounds like clicks, pops, or gunshots work best).
3.  **Tweak:**
    *   Adjust the **MASTER VOLUME** to your preference.
    *   Use the **PITCH FX** sliders to change the tone of your clicks.
        *   *Slide Left (0.5x):* Deeper / Slow-motion effect.
        *   *Slide Right (2.0x):* Higher / Snappy effect.
4.  **Hide:** Click the **X** button at the top right.
    *   The app **does not close**. It minimizes to your System Tray (the little arrow `^` near your clock).
5.  **Close:** To fully quit the app, right-click the **Blue Circle Icon** in your tray and select **Quit**.
